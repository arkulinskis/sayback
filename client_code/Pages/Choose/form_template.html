Great at the app. Stuck in the conversation?

If you have a 300-day streak and still freeze when someone actually talks to you, it isn't you. Most language apps train you to recognise words on a screen: tap the right picture, match the pairs, type the missing word. Conversation needs something else: pulling the phrase out of your own head and saying it out loud, before the moment passes. That's the only thing Spoken Miles does.

Questions

How is this different from Duolingo?

Duolingo is a fun way to build vocabulary and a daily habit, and lots of people love it. But most of the time you're reading and tapping, not speaking. Spoken Miles is all speaking: in every lesson, hundreds of phrases come out of your own mouth, timed so they move into long-term memory. Use both if you like. Spoken Miles is the one that gets you talking.

Is it safe to use while driving?

It's audio only and never asks you to look at anything. Speaking out loud is like talking to a passenger. Keep your attention on the road; if traffic gets busy, just listen, or pause. Car mode gives you one huge play button and nothing else.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Save a lesson, a pack of five, a level or the whole course to your phone, and it plays with no signal at all: tunnels, the countryside, a car park. Or stream it if your phone is short on space.

Do I pay every month?

No. You pay once and every lesson you bought is yours for life, with no subscription and nothing to cancel.

Can I give it as a present?

Yes. Buy it as a gift, add a message, then print the card or send the link. They open it with a free account and it's theirs for life.
