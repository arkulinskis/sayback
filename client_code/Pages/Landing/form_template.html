Apps make you tap. Sayback makes you talk.

You can recognise a thousand words on a screen and still freeze when a real person asks you something. Recognising isn't speaking. The only way to learn to speak is to speak: pulling the phrase out of your own memory, in the pause, before you hear the answer.

That moment of effort is what locks it in. In the 1960s, the linguist Dr. Paul Pimsleur showed that a phrase recalled at growing intervals (seconds, then minutes, then days) moves into long-term memory. Sayback times every phrase that way. You don't schedule anything. The lessons do it for you.

Questions

Is it safe to use while driving?

Sayback is audio only and never asks you to look at anything. Speaking out loud is like talking to a passenger. Keep your attention on the road; if traffic gets busy, just listen, or pause. Car mode in the app gives you one huge play button and nothing else.

Which Spanish does it teach?

Clear, neutral Spanish understood everywhere, using “ustedes” rather than Spain-only “vosotros”. The culture notes cover Spain, Mexico and South America, and Level 5 adds regional flavour.

How long until I can have a conversation?

By the end of Level 1 (about six weeks of workday commutes) you can handle greetings, cafés, shops, directions, hotels, the pharmacy, small talk and invitations. Real conversations grow through Levels 2 and 3.

Who are the voices?

Natural-sounding AI voices: a woman and a man for each language, so you hear how phrases change with who is speaking (“cansado”, “cansada”). That's also how we keep the price this low.

Do I need to do a lesson every day?

One a day works best, because sleep is when memory sets. Missed a day? Just carry on. Every lesson reviews earlier phrases for you.

Can I repeat a lesson?

Yes, and if a lesson felt hard, you should. A good rule: move on when you get about 80% of the answers out loud.

How do I get the lessons?

They unlock in your Sayback library the moment you pay. Play them in the app (it remembers where you stopped, and works from your lock screen and car), or download the MP3s and transcripts for any music app, CarPlay or a USB stick.
