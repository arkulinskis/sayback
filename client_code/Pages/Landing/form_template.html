Apps make you tap. Spoken Miles makes you talk.

You can recognise a thousand words on a screen and still freeze when a real person asks you something. Recognising isn't speaking. The only way to learn to speak is to speak: pulling the phrase out of your own memory, in the pause, before you hear the answer.

That moment of effort is what locks it in. In the 1960s, the linguist Dr. Paul Pimsleur showed that a phrase recalled at growing intervals (seconds, then minutes, then days) moves into long-term memory. Spoken Miles times every phrase that way. You don't schedule anything. The lessons do it for you.

Questions

How is this different from Duolingo?

Duolingo is a fun way to build vocabulary and a daily habit, and lots of people love it. But most of the time you're reading and tapping, not speaking. Spoken Miles is all speaking: in every lesson, hundreds of phrases come out of your own mouth, timed so they move into long-term memory. Use both if you like. Spoken Miles is the one that gets you talking.

Is it safe to use while driving?

Spoken Miles is audio only and never asks you to look at anything. Speaking out loud is like talking to a passenger. Keep your attention on the road; if traffic gets busy, just listen, or pause. Car mode in the app gives you one huge play button and nothing else.

Which Spanish does it teach?

Clear, neutral Spanish understood everywhere, using “ustedes” rather than Spain-only “vosotros”. The culture notes cover Spain, Mexico and South America, and Level 5 adds regional flavour.

Does it work without a signal, with the screen off?

Yes. Save lessons to your phone (a pack of five, a level or the whole course) and they play with no signal. It keeps playing with the screen locked, and play, pause and skip work from the lock screen, your headphones and most car displays. Add Spoken Miles to your home screen and it opens like an app.

How long until I can have a conversation?

By the end of Level 1 (about six weeks of workday commutes) you can handle greetings, cafés, shops, directions, hotels, the pharmacy, small talk and invitations. Real conversations grow through Levels 2 and 3.

Who are the voices?

Natural-sounding AI voices: a woman and a man, so you hear how phrases change with who is speaking (“cansado”, “cansada”). That's also how we keep the price this low.

Is it really mine for life?

Yes. You pay once. The lessons stay in your library for as long as Spoken Miles exists, and the MP3s you download are yours to keep forever, on any device.
