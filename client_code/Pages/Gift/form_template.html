Gift questions

Does he need to be good with apps?

No. He opens the link (or types the code from the card), makes an account with his email, and presses play. In the car there's one big button. Lessons can also be downloaded as plain MP3s for a USB stick or CarPlay.

What if they already have a lesson or two?

No problem. The gift adds to whatever is in their library, and their progress carries on.

Can they get a refund or swap the language?

Before it's opened, write to us and we'll swap the language or refund you. After it's opened, the usual "Speak or it's free" guarantee applies for 30 days.

Does the gift expire?

No. Give it now for a birthday months away; it waits until they open it.
