Stripe Payment Links

  1. In Stripe, make one product + Payment Link for each line below (one-time price).
  2. In each link's settings, "After payment" > "Don't show confirmation page" > redirect to the address under its box.
  3. Paste the link (https://buy.stripe.com/...) in its box and press Save.

Stripe webhook (unlocks lessons the moment people pay)

Stripe > Developers > Webhooks > Add endpoint. Address below; events checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded and charge.refunded. Then copy its signing secret (whsec_...) into the Anvil editor: App Secrets > stripe_webhook_secret.
